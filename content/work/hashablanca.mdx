## Problem shape

Blockchain analytics systems become noisy quickly. The challenge is not only ingesting event volume. The harder problem is turning volatile, low-context data into views that operations and compliance teams can act on.

## Approach

Hashablanca centers the data pipeline first: event ingestion, transformation discipline, and interface clarity. The product surface is designed to reduce interpretation cost instead of increasing dashboard density.

## Delivery signal

The project demonstrates event-stream thinking, infrastructure-aware interface design, and the ability to map technical anomalies into workflows that non-specialist stakeholders can follow.

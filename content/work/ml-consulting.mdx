## Focus

Consulting work centers on the gap between model capability and production clarity. Teams often have an acceptable prototype and an unacceptable operating surface.

## What changes

The work typically improves inference boundaries, observability, deployment confidence, and the translation layer between technical model behavior and business-readable outputs.

## Why it matters

Strong ML delivery is rarely blocked by model code alone. It is blocked by unclear ownership boundaries, fragile operations, and decisions that cannot be defended under scrutiny. The consulting offer targets exactly that layer.
